Common questions

Things you might be wondering.

What if I already know who it is from context clues in the chat?

That is up to you. The system protects your photo and name — what you share in conversation is yours to control. Most people find that talking first actually makes the potential awkwardness easier, not harder.

What happens if I reveal and the other person is someone I know?

The reveal is mutual, private, and not broadcast anywhere. Only the two of you see the result. If it is awkward, it stays between you — it does not appear on a timeline or affect anyone else.

Can someone screenshot my photo during the 3-minute window?

We cannot prevent screenshots technically, but the photo is never saved to our servers after the window closes, and the reveal only happens when both people have already agreed. This is designed for people who are genuinely interested — not for bad actors.

Is this only for romantic connections?

It is built with romantic intent in mind — the reveal flow and safety features are designed for that context. But the conversations themselves are just conversations. Some turn into friendships. That is fine too.
